# Smart Health & Medical History Tracker + Wellness Analytics System (SHM-WAS)

> A simple yet powerful Python-based system to **store, manage, and analyze patient health data digitally**.

---

## Overview

The **Smart Health & Medical History Tracker + Wellness Analytics System (SHM-WAS)** is designed to replace traditional paper-based medical records with a **digital, efficient, and intelligent system**.

It helps users:
- Store complete medical history   
- Track vital health parameters   
- Analyze wellness using BMI & health metrics   
- Access records anytime, anywhere 

---

## Aim of the Project

-  Digitally store patient records  
-  Reduce paperwork and manual errors  
-  Provide quick and easy data access  
-  Perform automatic health analysis  
-  Improve health awareness with insights  

---

## Features

-  Add & manage patient details  
-  Store medical history (disease, medicine, allergies)  
-  Track vitals (BP, Sugar, Weight, Height)  
-  Automatic BMI calculation  
-  Wellness analytics with health suggestions  
-  View complete patient report  

---

## BMI Calculation


| Category     | BMI Range |
|--------------|----------|
| Underweight  | < 18.5   |
| Normal       | 18.5–24.9|
| Overweight   | 25–29.9  |
| Obese        | ≥ 30     |
